Frozen Shoulder Treatment

The main symptoms caused by Frozen Shoulder, or Adhesive Capsulitis, would be initial pain in the joint, along with decreased mobility. Anyone, at any age, can suffer from Frozen Shoulder, though it is most commonly seen in people 40-70 years old, most of them women. It is normally caused by injury, misuse, lack of use, myocardial infarction, and upper torso surgery (arm and shoulder surgery or mastectomy). Any of these can cause the joint to tighten, which decreases mobility. Fortunately, our Chiropractors can help resolve Frozen Shoulder problems. With in-office physiotherapy and exercises can help decrease pain and increase range of motion! Call us today so you can end the pain and suffering from Frozen Shoulder.
