Now you can relieve the pain and inflammation of the heel associated with plantar fasciitis. Our new FDA market cleared laser has been proven in al tests to effectively treat this painful condition. In the past, only invasive therapeutic procedures were able to target the 10 percent of chronic plantar fasciitis cases unresponsive to non-operative therapy. Medical professionals have trusted low-level laser therapy (LLLT) for treating various conditions for over 50 years and are increasingly turning to the useful benefits of LLLT to manage conditions like plantar fasciitis more safely and effectively.

NOTHING ELSE LIKE IT

The FX 635 heel inflammation relief laser uses innovative low-level laser technology to treat heel pain. It’s designed to reduce inflammation and pain by stimulating the foot’s muscles, ligaments and nerves. The body absorbs the light energy, which encourages the cell’s mitochondria to produce more ATP and promotes natural healing within the cell. Because the laser merely stimulates the cells to promote healing naturally, the FX 635 provides heel pain relief with no adverse side effects.

Unlike traditional lasers, the FX 635 uses extremely low levels of light, allowing patients to enjoy the benefits of pain and inflammation reduction without the side effects associated with traditional lasers and surgery. Users remain awake throughout the entire plantar fasciitis laser treatment and experience no burning, scarring or downtime, allowing them to get on with the rest of their day as soon as the session is over.
